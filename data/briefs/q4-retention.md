# Q4 Retention Campaign — Brief

**Source:** brief-q4-retention
**Status:** live
**Objective:** Win back lapsed buyers (no purchase in 90+ days) and lift repeat-purchase rate.

## Audience
- Segment: customers with ≥1 purchase, last order 90–365 days ago.
- Exclusions: active subscribers, refund-in-last-30-days.

## Channels
- Email (primary), on-site banner, retargeting.

## Primary KPI
Repeat-purchase rate within 30 days of send. Secondary: revenue per recipient.

## Offer
15% win-back code, single use, 14-day expiry.

## Notes
Creative leans on "we saved your favorites." Hero image A/B tested — see the
October homepage hero experiment.
